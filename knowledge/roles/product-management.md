# Role Family Guide: Product Management

## 1. Industry Conventions & ATS Expectations
* **Format Priority**: Reverse-chronological, emphasizing product outcomes, revenue/business metrics, and cross-functional leadership over feature checklists.
* **High-Signal Evidence**:
  - Business impact: ARR growth, net revenue retention (NRR), conversion rate lifts (A/B testing), churn reduction, ARPU growth.
  - Discovery & execution: User research interviews conducted, PRD/spec delivery, sprint velocity, 0-to-1 launches.
  - Cross-functional leadership: Squad size (engineers, designers, data scientists), executive stakeholder alignment.

## 2. Keywords & Core Competencies
* **Disciplines**: Product-Led Growth (PLG), Enterprise Roadmapping, Pricing & Packaging, User Discovery, TAM/SAM, OKRs
* **Tools & Analytics**: SQL, Mixpanel, Amplitude, Segment, PostHog, Jira, Linear, Figma

## 3. Grounded Tailoring Rules
* Align product terminology (e.g. PLG vs Enterprise Sales-Led) with the hiring company's actual business model.
* Connect technical understanding to candidate's verified API or architecture knowledge.
* Ground all revenue and conversion claims in candidate's `VERIFIED_ACHIEVEMENTS.md`.
