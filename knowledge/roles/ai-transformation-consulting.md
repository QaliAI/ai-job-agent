# AI Strategy & Transformation

## What this lane means
Roles that translate AI capabilities into business workflows, adoption plans, implementation roadmaps, and measurable operating value.

## Common titles
- AI Transformation Consultant
- AI Strategy Consultant
- AI Adoption Lead
- AI Implementation Lead
- Fractional Chief AI Officer
- AI Automation Consultant

## High-value evidence
- Converting business problems into practical AI use cases
- Executive or team workshops
- Workflow mapping and automation
- Vendor/model/tool selection
- Prototypes that become operating systems
- ROI, time saved, revenue impact, or process improvement
- Change management and adoption

## Important distinction
Do not equate research-model expertise with transformation expertise. This lane rewards business translation, implementation, and adoption evidence.
