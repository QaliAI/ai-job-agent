# AI Product & Solutions Builder

## What this lane means
Hands-on roles that turn ambiguous business needs into usable AI-enabled products, prototypes, applications, automations, and internal tools.

## Common titles
- AI Product Builder
- Applied AI Engineer
- AI Solutions Engineer
- Product Engineer, AI
- AI Prototyping Engineer
- AI Product Lead

## High-value evidence
- Shipping web applications and internal tools
- LLM/agent workflows connected to real systems
- API and database integration
- Rapid prototyping and iteration with users
- Product judgment, UX tradeoffs, and deployment
- Reliability, security, observability, and testing

## Important distinction
Do not over-credit model research, CUDA, or distributed training unless the candidate has verified evidence. This lane is about shipping useful AI software.
