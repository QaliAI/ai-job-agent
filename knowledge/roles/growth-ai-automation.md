# Growth, Marketing & AI Automation

## What this lane means
Roles combining growth strategy, marketing operations, CRM/revenue systems, automation, analytics, and applied AI.

## Common titles
- Fractional CMO
- Head of Growth
- Growth Systems Lead
- AI Marketing Lead
- Marketing Automation Consultant
- Revenue Operations / RevOps AI Lead

## High-value evidence
- CRM architecture and lifecycle automation
- Lead generation and speed-to-lead systems
- Funnel conversion and pipeline reporting
- Paid/organic growth strategy
- AI-assisted research, personalization, and content operations
- Multi-location or service-business growth
- Cross-functional execution across marketing, sales, and technology

## Important distinction
Do not reduce this lane to content creation. Strong matches own systems, measurement, and revenue outcomes.
