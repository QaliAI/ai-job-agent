# Role Family Guide: Growth, Marketing, Sales & Operations

## Marketing & Growth
* **High-Signal Metrics**: Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), Lifetime Value (LTV), Pipeline Generated ($M), Marketing Qualified Leads (MQLs), Return on Ad Spend (ROAS), SEO organic traffic growth %.
* **Core Tooling**: HubSpot, Marketo, Google Ads, GA4, Webflow, Salesforce, SEO tools (Ahrefs/Semrush).

## Sales & Go-To-Market
* **High-Signal Metrics**: Quota attainment % (e.g. 140% of quota), Total Closed-Won ARR ($M), Average Contract Value (ACV), Sales Cycle Length (days reduced), Win Rate %.
* **Core Competencies**: MEDDPICC, Enterprise Account Planning, Outbound Prospecting, Contract Negotiation.

## Operations & Business Strategy
* **High-Signal Metrics**: Operational cost reduction %, Process throughput acceleration, Error rate reduction, Vendor cost savings, SLA compliance rate %.
* **Core Competencies**: Process Re-engineering, Supply Chain Modeling, Cross-department OKR tracking, Financial Modeling.
